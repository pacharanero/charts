All guitar charts in the collection, following the Marcus Baw Charting System (MBCS) conventions. Each chart includes chord progressions, lyrics, and performance notes formatted for easy reading during live performance.

MBCS was built around one goal: get the whole song onto a single printed page, with chords placed inline in the lyrics rather than on a separate line above them, so timing is easier to judge at a glance while playing.

For the full background and the formal notation reference, see the [project README](https://github.com/pacharanero/charts/blob/main/README.md) and the [MBCS specification](https://github.com/pacharanero/charts/blob/main/spec/mbcs.md).

## Chart Features

**Inline chord notation** - Chords positioned *within* lyrics at correct timing

**Single page** - Charts are designed to fit on a single page when printed

Chord progressions are established in the first verse/section

Subsequent sections show lyrics only when chord patterns repeat

Special performance notes included where needed for timing and arrangement
