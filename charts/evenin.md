## T-Bone Walker

### VERSE 1

Evenin', every night you'll come and find me  
and you always come and remind me  
that my gal is gone  
Evenin', can't you see I'm deep in your power  
every minute seems like an hour  
Since my gal...she's gone,

Oh yes, I don't care if I don't awaken  
Oh Lord, since my gal...she's gone,
