# Mildred Won’t You Behave?

## The Bill Elliott Swing Orchestra

[C swing blues]  

Well I was minding my business when I got a call - desperate plea from my old friend Paul. He says “my cousin Mildred’s here from out of state, I gotta fix her up with a - blind date” **(Mildred won’t you behave?)**  

Now Paul said, Mildred here is a quiet mouse, A real shy type, hardly leaves the house. But I said “Paul I’m going to the dance tonight!  
Well, bring her down and I’ll be polite”.  
**(Mildred won’t you behave?)**  

[C#] Well I was feelin’ pretty good when I got to the dance,  
I checked out the prospects for new romance.  
But there was Paul with his gal in tow, all frizzy hair with a big plaid bow.  
I said to myself - “I’ll just say Hi, Waltz her around and then - Bye- Bye”.  

But to my surprise, she said “Greetings Gate! - Are you a solid sender? Let’s activate!” She took a Lindy correspondence course.  
In twelve easy lessons, guaranteed of course.  
**(Mildred won’t you behave?)**  

[D#] Well I was out of my depth, I knew in a hurry, With a box-step I learned from Arthur Murray. She grabbed his collar and she spun him ‘round  
She flipped him over and she knocked him down.  
**(Mildred won’t you behave?)**  

[C] Well Jenner was the first to cut in, Then Barnard took Millie for a test spin  
She Suzie Q’d and she Trucked on down, The cats all cheered as she went to town.  
**(Mildred won’t you behave?)**  

When LeMar threw her upside the ceiling the whole darn place was rockin’ and reelin’. She took the Gators on two-by-tow, she even danced with an Ickaroo.  
**(Mildred won’t you behave?)**  

Hoi hoi hoi Millie - now don’t you get silly  
Hoi hoi hoi Millie - oh, silly Millie
