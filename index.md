# Marcus Baw Guitar Charts

Welcome to my collection of guitar chord and lyric charts. These charts are formatted using the Marcus Baw Musical Charting System (MBCS), designed for easy reading during performance.

## Features

- **Single-page format** - All chord progressions and lyrics fit on one page
- **Inline chord notation** - Chords are positioned directly within the lyrics at the correct timing
- **Clear structure** - Song sections are clearly marked and organized
- **Performance-friendly** - Easy to read while playing, optimized for live performance

## Structure

The charts are organized alphabetically and include:

- Song title and artist
- Chord progressions with inline placement
- Verse, chorus, bridge, and other section markers
- Performance notes and directions

## Conventions (MBCS)

- **Chords** are in square brackets and bold: **[C]**, **[Am]**, **[F]**, **[G]**
- **Section markers** are bold and uppercase: **[VERSE]**, **[CHORUS]**, **[BRIDGE]**
- **Chord repetition** - Full chord notation shown in first verse/section, subsequent sections show lyrics only when chord pattern repeats
- **Performance notes** included where helpful for timing, stops, and changes

## About

These charts have been collected and arranged over many years, originally in .doc format, then Google Documents, and now converted to Markdown for better maintainability and interaction with modern tools.

The charts use trailing spaces for proper line breaks and are optimized for both digital viewing and printing.
